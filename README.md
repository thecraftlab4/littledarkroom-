# Little Darkroom

**A photobooth, Polaroid studio and scrapbook — developed right in your browser.**

Shoot or upload photos, drop them into retro photo strips and Polaroid frames, decorate them with stickers and washi tape, and keep them in scrapbook pages. No app, no sign-up, no uploads: your photos never leave your device.

---

## What you can make

### 🎞️ Photo strips
- 30+ strip layouts with 2 to 6 frames: classic black, Hollywood, film, zine, gingham, lace and more.
- Take the photos **one by one inside the strip** with the live camera, or pick them from your gallery.

### 📸 Polaroids
- 18 Polaroid styles, each with its own film look.

### 🎬 Photobooth session
- **8 guided poses** ("say cheese", "and twice shy", "peace out"…) with a 3-second countdown and a flash.
- Retake any pose with one tap.
- When you're done, your strips **print and drop out of the booth's slot** into the collection tray, just like a real machine.

### ✂️ Decorate
- 200+ stickers in sets: cute, quotes, washi tapes, film, food, botanical and doodles.
- Captions, dates, filters and fonts.

### 📒 Scrapbook studio
- 16 editable page templates: Vintage Memory Journal, Korean Memory Scrapbook, Night Out Junk Journal, Valentine Date Night and more.
- A full page editor: move, resize, rotate, layers, undo/redo and zoom.
- Photo crop, filters, brightness/contrast, borders (including a jigsaw puzzle cut) and captions.
- 18 papers: kraft, leopard, harlequin check, cork, linen, grid…
- Save pages on your device and download them as PNG or JPG.

---

## How to use
1. Open the site and press **Create**.
2. Choose **Photo Strip**, **Polaroid** or **Scrapbook**.
3. Pick a template, then shoot, upload or arrange.
4. Decorate, then **save as PNG/JPG** or share.

---

## Privacy
Everything happens inside your browser. Photos are never uploaded to any server. Saved scrapbooks are stored only on your own device.

---

## Camera tips
The live camera needs the site to be opened over **https**, which it is when hosted on GitHub Pages. If it says no camera was found:
- **Windows:** Settings → Privacy & security → Camera → turn on *Let desktop apps access your camera*.
- Check the laptop's camera key or lens shutter.
- Allow camera access from the icon in the browser's address bar, then press **Try again**.

Works best in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on desktop and mobile.

## About this repo
The whole site is a **single file**, `index.html`, with all its code, images, stickers and fonts packed inside. Hosting it is just:

1. Upload `index.html` to the repo.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**, choose branch `main` / root, and save.
